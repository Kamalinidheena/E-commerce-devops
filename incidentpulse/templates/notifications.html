Delivery Transparency & Telemetry

IncidentPulse performs authentic HTTP POST webhook deliveries with bounded retries and exponential backoff. A successful HTTP 200 response from your receiver confirms that your webhook gateway accepted the payload—it does not prove that an on-call engineer read the notification. If delivery fails or times out, the incident remains securely preserved in the database.
